# Sir.G Tutors — Product Requirements Document (PRD)

## 1. Project Overview
Sir.G Tutors is an online tutoring marketplace that connects students and parents with qualified tutors. The platform will launch in Pakistan and may expand worldwide in the future.

## 2. Vision
Make finding, offering, and accessing quality education easier, more transparent, and more affordable.

## 3. Target Users
- Students: Find tutors for school, college, university, and professional subjects.
- Parents/Guardians: Find suitable tutors for their children.
- Tutors: Create profiles, list subjects, describe qualifications, and receive tutoring inquiries.
- Admin: Review submissions, manage listings, and help maintain platform quality.

## 4. Goals
- Launch a functional MVP with a zero-cost development and hosting setup where possible.
- Validate demand in Pakistan before expanding internationally.
- Make tutor discovery simple and mobile-friendly.
- Collect tutor applications and student inquiries through forms.

## 5. MVP Features
### Public Website
- Home page with clear value proposition and calls to action.
- Browse Tutors page with searchable/filterable tutor listings when data is available.
- Tutor profile cards with name, subjects, education/qualifications, teaching mode, and contact/inquiry action.
- Subjects and education levels covering school, college, university, medical, and other professional subjects.
- How It Works page explaining the process for students and tutors.
- Become a Tutor page with a tutor application form.
- Find a Tutor page with a student/parent request form.
- About and Contact pages.
- FAQ section.

### Tutor Applications
- Full name, email, optional phone, city/country, subjects, education level, qualifications, experience, online/in-person preference, availability, and a short introduction.
- Consent to the privacy notice before submission.
- Clear confirmation message after successful submission.

### Student/Parent Requests
- Student or guardian name, contact email, optional phone, city, required subject, education level, online/in-person preference, preferred schedule, and additional requirements.
- Consent to the privacy notice before submission.
- Clear confirmation message after successful submission.

### Admin and Data Management
- Use a simple, low-cost method to review tutor applications and student requests.
- Publish tutor profiles only after review and approval.
- Do not display private contact details without permission.
- Provide a way to correct or remove submitted information.

## 6. Future Features — Not Required for MVP
- Student and tutor accounts with secure login.
- Tutor dashboards and profile editing.
- Real-time chat and notifications.
- Built-in video lessons and scheduling.
- Online payments, commissions, and subscriptions.
- Ratings, reviews, and verified tutor badges.
- Automated matching recommendations.
- International currencies, languages, and location filters.

## 7. Design Requirements
- Brand name: Sir.G Tutors.
- Style: Modern, trustworthy, welcoming, academic, and professional.
- Responsive design for mobile, tablet, and desktop.
- Clear navigation, readable typography, accessible contrast, and consistent buttons.
- Use simple, lightweight assets to keep the website fast.
- Avoid fake tutor profiles, fabricated reviews, and unsupported qualification claims.

## 8. Technical Requirements
- Build with a beginner-friendly, AI-assisted coding workflow.
- Prefer free tools for development, data collection, and initial hosting.
- Use a maintainable component-based structure.
- Validate forms and show clear success/error states.
- Protect personal information and restrict access to submissions.
- Keep secrets and API keys out of public frontend code.
- Use environment variables for any required secrets.
- Provide basic accessibility, SEO metadata, and error handling.

## 9. Success Metrics
- Website can be opened successfully on desktop and mobile.
- Tutor application form works from submission to review.
- Student request form works from submission to review.
- Initial tutors can be reviewed and approved manually.
- Early users can understand how to find or offer tutoring.
- Track visits, tutor applications, student requests, and successful connections where practical.

## 10. MVP Acceptance Criteria
- All main navigation links work.
- Home, tutor discovery, tutor application, student request, About, and Contact pages are available.
- Forms validate required fields and display confirmation/error messages.
- Submitted data reaches the chosen storage or form-management service.
- Tutor listings show only approved, genuine profiles.
- Layout works on mobile and desktop.
- Privacy information and contact details are visible.
- No paid service is required for the initial prototype unless explicitly approved.

## 11. Assumptions and Constraints
- Initial target market: Pakistan.
- Future target market: worldwide.
- All education levels and a broad range of subjects are in scope, including medical subjects.
- Initial budget: zero or near zero.
- The first release is an MVP, not a full-scale marketplace.
- Free plans may have usage limits, and domain registration or certain integrations may cost money.

## 12. Out of Scope for Initial Launch
- Guaranteed tutor credentials without verification.
- Automated payments or commission collection.
- Native mobile applications.
- Unmoderated public reviews.
- Guaranteed tutor availability or learning outcomes.

## 13. Recommended Launch Strategy
1. Build the website prototype.
2. Connect tutor and student forms to a free or free-tier data collection method.
3. Test submissions and mobile usability.
4. Invite a small group of genuine tutors to apply.
5. Manually review applications and publish approved profiles.
6. Test with students and parents in Pakistan.
7. Improve the service based on feedback before expanding.
